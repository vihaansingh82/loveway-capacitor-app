FAQs

Common questions

Is Loveway free to use? +
Yes! Loveway is completely free. Every feature — private chat, song dedication, announcements — is available at no cost.
Do I have to wait for approval? +
No. Once you verify your OTP and complete your profile, your account is active right away. Admins moderate afterwards and can block any account that breaks the rules.
Is my data safe? +
Absolutely. Your personal information is encrypted and never shared with advertisers or third parties. Privacy is at our core.
Can I delete my account? +
Yes, you can delete your account at any time from Settings. All your data including messages and dedications will be permanently removed.
What if I see inappropriate content? +
Use the Report button on any post, message or profile. Our team responds to reports within a few hours and takes action swiftly.
